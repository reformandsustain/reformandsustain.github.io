Welcome to Reform and Sustain

We are a social group for all earth lovers in Orange County, CA. Our mission is to provide a safe space for earth lovers and environmental activists to build community and share their eco optimism and anxiety.

In today’s political climate, it is easy to feel alone and to feel like you are the only one acknowledging the disastrous affects of climate change. More than ever, it is important to feel a sense of community and pull ourselves out of doomerism. So how do we do that?

Online hangouts and chat forums

Online hangouts: We hangout on Discord every third Tuesday of the month at 7:30pm PST. We use the hour to chat about any climate news, call to actions, volunteer opportunities, or discuss our next hangout.

Chat forum: We have several channels on Discord for various topics from plants/animals to positive/bummer news to RS/community events and more.

Support and highlight local environmental events

We highlight, support, and uplift other environmental organizations and their advocacy projects; we aim to connect our members to local organizations and encourage collaboration so we are united in our shared goal of increasing environmental action.

Foster fun community

We hold monthly social Zoom meetings and in-person, social events to bond as a group and increase our visibility and credibility as a community organization.

Find earth loving friends and be part of the climate movement

Fill out the sign-up form to get added on our Discord. Chat with us online, hangout with us during in-person socials, join us for volunteer events!
